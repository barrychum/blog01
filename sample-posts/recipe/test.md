Three Cup Chicken (San Bei Ji) is arguably one of Taiwan's most famous culinary exports. The name comes from the traditional recipe which called for **one cup of sesame oil, one cup of soy sauce, and one cup of rice wine**. 

Don't worry—modern recipes (including this one!) scale that ratio down so it isn't swimming in grease, but the intense, savory-sweet profile remains exactly the same.

### Why You'll Love This Recipe
* **The Aromatics:** Sizzling whole garlic cloves and ginger in dark sesame oil creates an incredible base flavor.
* **The Basil Finish:** The fresh Thai basil tossed in at the very end is non-negotiable—it completely transforms the dish.
* **One-Pan Meal:** It comes together in a single wok or cast-iron skillet in under 30 minutes.
